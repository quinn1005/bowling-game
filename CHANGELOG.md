## v1.2.1

### Changed
- **Cursed charms are now hexed, not doubled.** Corrupted charms already trigger twice, so curses do something different: each charm that can be cursed has its own **hexed form** with a new name and a twisted effect, a bigger upside with a drawback built in. A few examples:
  - Strike Out becomes **Strike or Bust**: ×2 Mult on strikes, ×0.5 Mult on anything else.
  - Chalk Bag becomes **Ash Bag**: +10 Points per pin your first ball knocks down, -8 Points per pin your later rolls knock down.
  - Underdog becomes **Lone Wolf**: ×1.5 Mult per empty charm slot, -2 Mult for every other charm you hold.
  - Statue becomes **Medusa**: ×2 Mult if no pin wobbled, ×0.8 Mult if any did.
  - Gutter Punk becomes **Gutter Ghoul**: ×4 Mult on gutter frames and +2 Mult per pin in the gutter, -3 Mult on strikes.

  There are 28 hexes in all. **Lift the curse and the drawback is gone for good, but the hexed upside stays.** Only charms with a hexed form show up cursed in shops, and a hexed charm doesn't evolve.
- **Every achievement card has its own art** that matches its name: a roast turkey for Turkey Dinner, a snail with a bowling-ball shell for Slow Roller, a night owl for Night Owl, a wizard hat for Pinball Wizard, and so on for all 74 cards.
- Head to Head: the opponent descriptions no longer rank the three of them.

## v1.2.0

### Daily Boss Rush
- **A new mode on the title screen.** Every boss, back to back, with a Pro Shop between each fight. You start with a set build (two common charms, an uncommon, two extra balls, a scorecard and $12) instead of an empty bag.
- **Seeded for the day.** Everyone who plays on the same day gets the same build, the same bosses and the same shops, so you can compare how deep you got. Your best depth for the day shows on the button.
- Everything is unlocked during a rush, so the shops can offer anything.

### Head to Head, now with a draft
- **Bowl against Quinn, Kaleb or Levi** on the same lane: you bowl a frame, then they bowl theirs, and you watch their throw.
- **Three rounds of 5 frames, with a draft before each round.** You and your opponent take turns picking from one shared pool of 4 charms, 2 balls and 2 items, 3 picks each. Whatever you leave, they can take. Who picks first alternates each round. If your slots are full, you pick something to swap out.
- **Tiered opponents, hardest first:**
  - **Quinn** drafts the smartest: tests each charm in every slot of the build to find real synergies, takes picks that are much better for you, and saves items for the right moment (an Extra Frame once behind or in the last round, and a Loaded Ball only when the boost is worth the charm it destroys).
  - **Kaleb** bowls a big wide swing, drafts the strongest-looking picks by rarity, keeps ×Mult charms at the end of the row, and saves items for the last round.
  - **Levi** bowls straight down the middle, drafts on a hunch (a fun ball is hard to pass up) and uses items the moment they come.
- The end screen shows the score for each round and the opponent's final build. Your record against each of them is kept.

### Cursed charms
- Shops sometimes sell a **cursed charm**. It triggers twice every frame, but you can't sell it and it carries a curse until you meet its condition: Heavy (smaller PERFECT zone, lifted by 6 PERFECTs), Leaky (-$1 a frame, lifted by 3 strikes), Dark (dark lane, lifted by 4 spares), Jinx (-3 Mult, lifted by a Turkey) or Chains (no re-racks, lifted by clearing 2 rounds). Lift it and it stays **blessed**.

### Ghost race
- **Challenge links now carry your frames.** Open a friend's link and their ghost races you frame by frame: a pill in the HUD shows their score at the same point and whether you're ahead or behind.

### Achievement cards
- **Every feat is now a collectable card**, with its own art and colour by category. See them in the Collection under "Card gallery". Cards you haven't earned show their back.

### Feel
- **Every ball type sounds different as it rolls:** heavy balls rumble low, light ones hiss, glass rings, rubber squeaks and metal clanks.
- **Camera follow:** the view leans in and follows the ball down the lane. You can turn it off in the menu.

## v1.1.0

### Endless mode is its own game now
After you beat the Gutter King, endless takes you through seven new alleys you can't see anywhere else, each with its own lane and two much harder bosses. Targets climb faster too.
- **The Bowl-o-Rama Wreck:** holes in the lane swallow your ball. *The Demolition Foreman* knocks a new hole every frame; *The Scrapper* hauls off a third of your knocked pins before they score.
- **Deep Sea Lanes:** slower throws and a current that pushes the pins. *The Kraken* grabs balls near the gutters; *The Angler* leaves you in the dark with one wandering lure light.
- **The Haunted Hall:** ghost pins phase in and out as Phantom pins. *The Poltergeist* flings your leftover pins to new spots; *The Banshee* scrambles your charm order every frame.
- **Volcano Lanes:** a ball that falls into a lava gutter burns up for the round. *The Magma King* burns the oil away faster every frame; *The Ash Cloud* hides the oil pattern on the lane and the lane map.
- **Sky Lanes:** no gutters, just a drop-off. *The Wind Spirit* sends gusts that change direction mid-roll; *The Thunderbird* strikes 2 knocked pins back up.
- **The Clockwork Tower:** brass gears turn across the lane. *The Clockmaker* runs every slider twice as fast, and faster each frame. *Old Father Time* has a 6-second shot clock on every throw (the ball rolls as the sliders stand when it runs out), and each frame one of your charms falls asleep.
- **The Void** (repeats forever): the lane edges pull your ball outward. *The Hollow King* steals your two best charms and a trophy for the match; *The Echo* halves any frame that doesn't beat your best frame from the last round.
- **Endless Medals:** one for every 3 endless alleys, worth XP and three medal-only cosmetics (Void swirl trail, Obsidian Trim pins, Void lane).
- **Corrupted charms:** endless shops sometimes sell a corrupted charm. It triggers twice every frame but costs $1 a frame and sells for nothing.
- **Endless depth board:** how deep Kaleb, Levi and Quinn got, on the Feats screen.

### New charms (each unlocked by a themed feat)
- **Glass Cannon** (×4 Mult, shatters on an open frame): win a round with no open or gutter frames.
- **Pocket Watch** (Mult for slow, controlled throws): strike with a ball that takes over 1.3 s to reach the pins.
- **Ricochet King** (×1.1 per wall bang and pin-on-pin hit): 10 of them in one frame.
- **Bargain Bin** ($1 off and an extra shop item): buy 3 things in one shop visit.
- **Perfectionist's Curse** (×3 on all-PERFECT frames, ×0.5 on a miss): 3 all-PERFECT frames in a row.

- **Rack Attack** (+5 Points per pin in your rack): fill all 15 pin spots.
- **Taxidermy Trophy** (+3 Mult per trophy): hold 4 trophies.
- **Second Wind** (one more frame when you'd lose): win a round on its very last frame.

### New balls and equipment
- **Anchor Ball:** nothing can push it off line after the arrows. Unlock: strike in Deep Sea Lanes.
- **Gyro Ball:** stops at the pins and spins in place, sweeping them. Unlock: 8 pins down within half a second.
- **Black Pearl:** hooks in the oil, straight on dry boards. Unlock: beat The Kraken.
- **Grenade Ball:** explodes 3 seconds after release. Unlock: 3 explosions in one frame.
- **Lucky Sweatband** (+1 re-rack, smaller PERFECT zone). Unlock: use every re-rack in a round and still win.

### New events
- **Pro Shop Sale** (next shop half price, 3 items), **The Lucky Fountain** ($5 for a 1 in 3 shot at a Rare charm), **The Lost Bowler** (blind ball trade), **The Regulars' Bet** (Kaleb, Levi and Quinn pay out as you go further) and **The Back Room Card Game** (stake a charm on a coin flip).

## v1.0.0

### Every throw feels bigger
- **"Will it fall?"** When the last pin or two teeter after the ball has gone by, time slows, the screen closes in and a heartbeat plays. If it drops: a roar, a flash and "DOWN!". If it holds: the pin taunts you with "NOPE".
- **Pocket hits freeze the frame** for a split second with a deep thump before the pins explode out.
- **Streak names, like at a real alley:** Double, Turkey, Hambone, Five-Bagger, Wild Turkey, Seven- and Eight-Bagger, Golden Turkey. Each one is bigger and louder, with confetti from a Turkey up.
- **Scoring hits harder:** ×Mult steps land with a bass hit and jolt the score panel. Tap the lane to skip the count-up.
- **Groove music:** the band builds as your PERFECT streak grows (drums at 3, bass at 5, a lead melody at 8).

### Winning a round pays off
- **Overkill:** beat the target by 1.5×, 2× or 3× for bonus cash. Past 2× the scorecard catches fire.
- **Bonus balls:** every unused frame (up to 3) becomes a bonus throw at a rack of gold pins. Every 4 pins pay $1, and a strike pays $1 more.
- **Boss trophies:** beat a boss and pick 1 of 3 trophies themed on that boss: 48 in all, like the Janitor's Mop Bucket, the Bookie's Loaded Dice or the Gutter King's Stolen Crown. They sit on their own shelf (the cup in the header), never take a charm slot and can't be stolen.
- **Charm evolutions:** 14 charms evolve once you've done their thing enough, e.g. Hot Streak becomes Inferno after 3 Turkeys and League Jacket becomes Varsity Jacket after 12 frames. The charm's description shows its progress.

### Surprises
- **Jackpot pin:** about 1 rack in 150, a sparkling pin appears. Knock it down and the lane TV spins a slot machine.
- **The Regulars drop by** between rounds: Quinn, Kaleb or Levi with a gift, a dare ($10 for a first-frame strike) or a tip.
- **New lane events:** a birthday party (balloons on the pins, +4 Points per pin), blackout bowling (only the pins glow, ×1.5 Mult) and a runaway pin that slides around until you hit it ($2).

### Progress between runs
- **Bowler level:** every run earns XP, shown on the end screen, up to level 50.
- **The Locker:** 43 cosmetics unlocked by bowler level: 12 ball trails, 11 pin skins, 9 lane finishes and 11 hats for the crowd. Looks only, no gameplay effect.
- **Collection book:** every ball, charm, pin type and trophy you've found, with silhouettes for the rest.
- **Highlight reel:** watch your run's 3 best frames from the end screen.

### Play with friends
- **Beat the Regulars:** Kaleb, Levi and Quinn have best frames on the Feats screen. Beat one and they react on the lane TV.
- **Challenge a friend:** the end screen copies a link with your run's seed, so a friend plays the same lanes and shops.

### Balance
- Targets now climb faster from alley to alley, to make up for trophies, bonus balls and evolutions. The balance bot plays with all of them, and good and great players clear about as many rounds as before (about 4 and 6).

## v0.5.0

### New
- **Ball return.** Your balls now sit on a ball return at the right side of the approach. Tap a ball on the return to throw it (number keys still work). The old ball buttons under the lane are gone, which leaves more room for the lane.
- **Jammed balls.** Now and then (6% of throws) the ball you just threw gets stuck in the return. It flashes STUCK in the hood, and you have to throw a different ball before it comes back.
- **Pinsetter fumbles.** Once in a while (5% of frames) the pinsetter fumbles after your first ball and drops one or two of the pins you left standing. They count as knocked down by that ball, so a fumble can even turn into a strike.
- **Lane TV.** A TV beside the lane plays short pixel cartoons: pins flying off and a victory dance for strikes, two balls high-fiving for a Double, a strutting turkey for a Turkey, a pin waving a white flag (then fainting) or a fist pump for spares, a pin doing the splits for split conversions, and the 7 and 10 pins hugging for a 7-10.
- **Oil breaks down.** Every ball strips oil from the boards it rolls over and carries a little past the end of the pattern. Throw the same line over and over and it dries out: the boards lose their blue sheen and turn brown, and your ball starts hooking earlier. The lane is freshly oiled every round.

## v0.2.0

### New
- **New throwing controls.** Each throw is three steps: drag to set your starting spot, drag to set the angle, then tap to stop the sweeping power bar. The green PERFECT zone gives a hard, straight throw; going past it makes the ball wobble off line.
- Equipment reworked around the new controls: Steady Glove slows the power bar, Wrist Brace widens the PERFECT zone, Snap Wrist stops overpowered throws from wobbling, and shoes change how far and how finely you can angle.
